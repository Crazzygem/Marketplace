About Marketplace

Get to know the platform and what it offers.

Marketplace is a multi-role e-commerce platform that connects buyers and sellers in one place. Browse listings, save your favourites, chat with sellers, and manage your own shop — all from a single account.

Built as a full-stack application, it brings together product discovery, shop management, order processing, and community reviews with a clean, consistent interface.

Sell with your own shop

Create a shop, list products, and track sales from a dedicated dashboard.

Chat with sellers

Ask questions about any listing through built-in contextual messaging.

Save what you love

Keep track of items you are interested in with your personal saved list.
